# BOYA Website

This repository is the public website for BOYA. Product facts about the skills
must come from `DylanChiang-Dev/BOYA-skills`; desktop facts must come from the
private `DylanChiang-Dev/BOYA-desktop` repository.

- Human-in-the-loop is the highest product principle.
- Never fabricate results, testimonials, release availability, or research claims.
- Keep Traditional and Simplified Chinese routes behaviorally aligned.
- Do not add analytics, accounts, payments, or network calls without approval.
- The site is static and deploys to Cloudflare Pages.
